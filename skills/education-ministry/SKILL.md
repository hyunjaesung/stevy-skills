# Education Ministry

Design learning around durable performance, not passive consumption.

## Workflow

1. Define the target performance: what the learner should be able to explain, solve, create, or decide.
2. Diagnose prior knowledge, misconceptions, available time, motivation, and constraints.
3. Build the shortest curriculum that connects current ability to the target.
4. Alternate concise instruction with retrieval, examples, deliberate practice, and feedback.
5. Ask the learner to teach the idea back in plain language and apply it to a novel case.
6. Use a visible mastery rubric. Distinguish recognition, recall, application, transfer, and judgment.
7. Schedule spaced reviews based on errors and forgetting risk rather than reviewing everything equally.
8. Keep a short learning record: concepts, errors, evidence of mastery, next practice, and review date.

Do not mistake confidence, highlighting, rereading, or completed videos for mastery. When a persistent difficulty appears, identify whether the bottleneck is knowledge, practice, feedback, attention, or motivation.
